# Changes

Each version's notes appear in the app under **Settings → About & updates** when it's available.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-18

- **Opt-in is now the recommended way to build your list.** We suggest creating a tag in ChurchSuite called **Emergency Hub**, adding it only to people who have agreed to be part of the plan, and ticking just that tag. On a new ChurchSuite connection it's ticked automatically.
- **Settings → Data & privacy** explains the opt-in tag, marks it in the tag list, and warns you if it's missing, not ticked, or ticked alongside broader tags such as Attender or Partner.
- The **Data Protection Pack** now recommends an opt-in, with consent as the suggested lawful basis, and includes ready-to-use wording for inviting people to join the plan. The privacy notice, risk assessment and checklists have been updated to match.
- The quick start guide and FAQ cover opting in, including a new question: "Do people need to agree to be on the list?"
- Switching an existing list to the opt-in tag removes everyone who hasn't opted in, along with their statuses and notes. The app asks you to confirm first and takes a backup.

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-14

- **Find a person** is now at the top of the left-hand panel.
- The map's layers button (for switching to satellite) has a clearer icon and is easy to see in dark mode, as are the zoom buttons.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-14

- A tidier title bar: all the buttons now live in one **Menu** in the top right corner, grouped into Contacts (sync, upload, export), Planning (hub groups, table view, not on map, changes report), and Settings, dark mode, lock and help.
- The menu works with the keyboard too: the arrow keys move through it and Esc closes it.
- When a new version is available, the orange dot now appears on the Menu button.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-14

- If Emergency Hub is already open in another window, starting it again now says so (and opens it in your browser) instead of showing a "page can't be found" error. If an older copy is still running, it tells you to close that window first.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-14

- A tidier folder. The download now holds just an **Emergency Hub** shortcut with its own icon, an **app** folder (the program) and, once you've started it, a **data** folder (your contacts, settings and backups). Back up the data folder and you've got everything.
- `config.json` now lives in the data folder too.
- Once you've started it, you can copy the Emergency Hub shortcut to your desktop.
- Copies of version 1.0.0 that install this update keep their old folder layout and carry on working. To get the new layout, download the Windows zip, unzip it, and move your `data` folder and `config.json` across (put `config.json` inside `data`).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14

The first public release.

- Map your church's address list, with everyone at the same address shown as one dot, coloured by their most important status.
- Plan a hub-and-spoke network: the church, then hub coordinators, then the people nearest each of them. Larger churches can use up to five levels of hubs.
- Use the statuses that suit you (Vulnerable, Single occupancy, Leader and so on), with your own names, colours and order.
- Bring contacts in from a CSV file or straight from ChurchSuite, with a report of what changed each time.
- Table view for ticking statuses quickly and finding everyone within a set distance of a postcode.
- Printed call lists for each hub, with a map, tick boxes and a reminder when the paper copies are out of date.
- Encrypt the data with a passphrase, and create a Data Protection Pack explaining your responsibilities under UK law.
- The street map you've looked at is kept on the computer, so it still works if the internet goes down.
- A demo church full of made-up people, so you can show the system safely.
- Built-in help, with a quick start guide you can print and answers to common questions.
- The app can now update itself: **Settings → About & updates**.
- The Windows download comes with everything it needs, including Python, so there's nothing else to install.
